Your Advocate in Criminal Law

Daniel Marcovitch is dedicated to protecting your rights and guiding you through the justice system with integrity, clarity, and commitment. With extensive experience defending clients facing a wide range of criminal charges — from minor offences to serious allegations — Daniel combines in-depth legal knowledge with a client-first approach.

He understands the stress and uncertainty that come with criminal charges and takes the time to listen, explain your options, and build a strong, personalized defence. Whether negotiating resolutions or representing you in court, Daniel is a steady and strategic advocate who fights for fair outcomes and treats every client with respect.
